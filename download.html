Renux Nightly build Experimental

Renux in development, built by GitHub Actions from the latest source every day at 18:00 Brasília time (21:00 UTC). A nightly build has no version: each one is named by its day and its commit. They are for testing: things change, and can break, from one build to the next.

The first release of Renux. Aurora is in development, and there is nothing to download yet. Until it comes out, the nightly builds are its preview.
