  3. Your device, your rulesNo personal data is collected, and no age verification is done or will be.

Origin

Renux is Renan + Unix. Its creator, Renan Lucas Vieira Hilário, uses Linux and the BSDs, and saw how many toolchains and dependencies it takes to build them. Zig, his language of choice, compiles C, C++ and Zig for any machine, and it is a single binary. So the question came: what if an operating system were easy to maintain, portable, and built on many systems at the cost of Zig alone? Renux is the answer, and it is meant to be a technology anyone can take up.
